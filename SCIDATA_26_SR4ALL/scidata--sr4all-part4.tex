\section*{Technical Validation}

This section evaluates the technical quality of the extracted methodological fields released in \webisSRCorpus{}. The main validation question is whether values retained after the verify-then-repair pipeline are supported by the source full text and can therefore be trusted for downstream analysis. Since these fields are available only for the subset of reviews with accessible full texts, the section focuses on the quality of the extracted layer rather than on the completeness of the full corpus as a whole.

The validation is designed to support three kinds of claims. First, non-null extracted values should be factually supported by the review text from which they were derived. Second, the paper should describe how often the extraction pipeline abstains, so that absence of a value is not confused with evidence that the underlying review omitted the information. Third, the section should identify the main situations in which parsing, grounding, or verification remain unreliable. Together, these points define what technical trust in this dataset means: users should understand which extracted values are likely to be reliable, which fields are sparse because the pipeline abstains, and which limitations remain.

The section does not aim to serve as a retrieval demonstration or as a benchmark of downstream task effectiveness. In particular, it does not try to show that normalized queries reproduce original database behavior, nor that the corpus yields strong retrieval performance in a specific search system. Such use-case material belongs in \textit{Usage Notes} or later application-oriented work. The purpose of \textit{Technical Validation} here is narrower and more fundamental: to justify the reliability and limits of the released extracted data.
