# ISSUE-07: Specify the manual extraction-review protocol

## What to do

- Define how human review of sampled extracted values will be carried out.
- State the scoring criteria and review procedure clearly.

## How to do it

 - Define which fields are reviewed and what counts as correct, partially correct, unsupported, unverifiable, or missing.
 - Decide whether one or multiple annotators are involved and how disagreements are resolved.
- Write the protocol in `SCIDATA_26_SR4ALL/scidata--sr4all-part4.tex`.

## Definition of done

- The manual validation process is reproducible from the paper text.
- The scoring categories are explicit.
- Reviewers can understand how human judgments of extraction correctness were produced.
