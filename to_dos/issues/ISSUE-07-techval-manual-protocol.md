# ISSUE-07: Specify the manual review protocol

## What to do

- Define how human review of sampled records will be carried out.
- State the scoring criteria and review procedure clearly.

## How to do it

- Define which fields are reviewed and what counts as correct, partially correct, unsupported, or missing.
- Decide whether one or multiple annotators are involved.
- Write the protocol in `SCIDATA_26_SR4ALL/scidata--sr4all-part4.tex`.

## Definition of done

- The manual validation process is reproducible from the paper text.
- The scoring categories are explicit.
- Reviewers can understand how human judgments were produced.
