# ISSUE-26: Decide how to handle the retrieval demonstration

## What to do

- Decide whether the retrieval demonstration stays in the paper, shrinks substantially, moves to another section, or is removed.
- Ensure the paper does not drift back toward a SIGIR-style evaluation narrative.

## How to do it

- Compare the current demonstration material against Scientific Data expectations and the local plan.
- Decide what role, if any, it plays in data utility rather than paper results.
- Update manuscript structure accordingly.

## Definition of done

- There is one clear decision on the retrieval demonstration.
- The manuscript structure reflects that decision consistently.
- No orphaned references or mixed framing remain.
