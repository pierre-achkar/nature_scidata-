# ISSUE-20: Add `Funding`

## What to do

- Add the `Funding` statement to the manuscript.
- Include all required funding or no-funding wording.

## How to do it

- Confirm the relevant grants, institutional support, or no-funding case.
- Add the section to the manuscript end matter.
- Keep it separate from acknowledgments.

## Definition of done

- The manuscript contains a `Funding` section.
- The wording is publication-ready.
- Funding information is not mixed into other sections.
