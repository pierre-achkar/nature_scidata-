# ISSUE-31: Prepare a revision-stage checklist

## What to do

- Prepare the revision-stage package checklist ahead of time.
- Cover the typical round-2 Scientific Data requirements.

## How to do it

- Include separate figure files, highlighted changes, response-to-reviewers material, and deposit/manuscript consistency checks.
- Base the checklist on the accepted `SDATA-23-nature-journal` process.
- Keep the list practical and submission-focused.

## Definition of done

- A concrete revision-stage checklist exists.
- The checklist reflects actual Scientific Data workflow needs.
- Later revision work will not start from scratch.
