# ISSUE-06: Define stratification variables for validation

## What to do

- Define how the validation sample should be stratified.
- At minimum, cover discipline, document length, and extraction outcome category if appropriate.

## How to do it

- Review the current corpus distributions.
- Choose stratification variables that expose meaningful variation in extraction difficulty and corpus coverage.
- Document the strata in `SCIDATA_26_SR4ALL/scidata--sr4all-part4.tex`.

## Definition of done

- The validation sampling strategy includes explicit strata.
- The chosen strata are justified in the manuscript.
- Reviewers can see that the sample is not biased toward easy cases.
