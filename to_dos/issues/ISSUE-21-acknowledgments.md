# ISSUE-21: Add `Acknowledgments` if needed

## What to do

- Decide whether an `Acknowledgments` section is needed and add it if appropriate.
- Keep it brief and policy-compliant.

## How to do it

- Check whether there are non-author contributors or institutional acknowledgments to include.
- Avoid duplicating funding material.
- Add the section to the manuscript end matter only if justified.

## Definition of done

- The manuscript either contains a concise `Acknowledgments` section or deliberately omits it.
- The decision is documented and consistent with journal expectations.
